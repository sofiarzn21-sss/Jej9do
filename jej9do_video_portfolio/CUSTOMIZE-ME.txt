jej9do — Video Portfolio
=======================

This is your customized HTML5 UP Strata starter site.

QUICK CUSTOMIZATION
1. Open index.html in a code editor (Visual Studio Code is recommended).
2. Replace the six sample project titles and descriptions in the Selected work section.
3. Replace each YOUR_VIDEO_ID_01 ... YOUR_VIDEO_ID_06 in the YouTube links with your actual video IDs.
   For Vimeo, replace each href with your Vimeo video URL.
4. Replace images/thumbs/01.jpg through 06.jpg with still frames from your own videos.
   Keep the same filenames and use JPG images for easiest replacement.
5. Replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with your real contact email address.
6. Replace images/avatar.jpg if you later decide to add a profile portrait (the current layout uses a wordmark).
7. The overall look is controlled by assets/css/custom.css. The font is DM Sans + Manrope (loaded from Google Fonts).

PREVIEW
- Extract the folder.
- Open index.html in a browser, or use the Live Server extension in VS Code.
- An internet connection is needed for Google Fonts and external YouTube/Vimeo links.

VIDEO LINKS
The six video links are intentionally placeholders. They will not play until you replace the example IDs with your own video URLs.
