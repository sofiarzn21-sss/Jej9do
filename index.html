WELCOME TO MY PORTFOLIO

Video is passion.
graphics is profession.

I’ve been working in this field since 2019: I started out editing video clips from movies, TV series and anime. At first on my phone, then on my laptop. Gradually, my passion grew and I began working in motion graphics, using 3D modeling and various types of text animation. In 2024, I took an extended break due to financial difficulties and a move. During that time, I practiced filming my own work and editing it into short films. My inspiration continued to spark new ideas and concepts that I would love to share with the world on a professional level!
